How it works

The calculation

For each skill: yearly volume × rollout pace × scenario multiplier gives the units used. Workday bills in whole blocks, so credits = ⌈(units − any free allowance) ÷ block size⌉ × credits per block.

Payroll missing-data analysis is billed per 10 worker records on every scheduled run. Metered API calls above your entitlement cost 60 credits per 10,000 calls.

Credits to buy = expected use − complimentary credits, plus your safety buffer, rounded up to the next 1,000. The cost is credits × price per credit.

Points to settle with the Workday account team

  • Running out. The FAQ says access continues and a negative balance comes off the next entitlement. Clause 1.g of the Policy says the customer must stop using Offers without cover. Confirm which applies in the order form.
  • Sana Learn. The FAQ says it is not on the credit model. The 5 Nov 2026 rate card prices narration at 30 credits a minute, with 432 free minutes a year and no charge until around 21 Nov 2026.
  • Sana base entitlement. Its size is not in the documents, so Sana Actions have no free allowance here.
  • Not generally available. Frontline mass shift adjustment may change or never ship.
  • Early Adopter. Data Cloud and MCP packaging is still being defined. The Pro and Core lines apply to legacy SKUs only.
  • BP Optimize. The one-off initial activation charge is excluded. Read it from the agent setup screen in a sandbox tenant.
